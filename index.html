Sidhu Steel Solar

Welcome to Sidhu Steel Solar, a company founded by Vikram Singh in 2020 with a vision to deliver world-class, innovative, and sustainable solutions in the steel and solar industry. As a trusted manufacturer, we specialize in high-quality solar structures, customized fabrication solutions, and architectural metalworks. Our commitment to precision engineering ensures that our products meet the highest industry standards, offering both durability and efficiency.

At Sidhu Steel Solar, we take pride in our ability to design and manufacture robust solar structures that enhance the efficiency and longevity of solar energy systems. Our expertise extends beyond solar structures to customized metal fabrication, serving diverse industries, including construction, infrastructure, and residential projects. With a customer-centric approach, we ensure that every project is tailored to meet specific needs while adhering to international quality standards.

Innovation and sustainability are at the heart of everything we do. As the demand for renewable energy solutions grows, we continue to develop advanced solar accessories that help businesses and individuals optimize energy consumption. Our fabrication services, including designer gates, grills, staircases, and high-grade materials, provide aesthetic and functional solutions that stand the test of time. Whether it's residential, commercial, or industrial applications, we deliver excellence in every project.

Our team of skilled professionals brings extensive experience in engineering, operations, sales, and electrical systems, ensuring seamless execution from design to installation. Led by Vikram Singh (CEO & Founder), Sonali Kapoor (Operational Director), Akashdeep Singh (Sales Marketing Head), and Harpreet Singh (Electrical Engineer), we work collaboratively to drive innovation and customer satisfaction. With a passion for quality and precision, our team remains dedicated to delivering cutting-edge solutions that exceed expectations.

Located in Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar, Punjab, we have successfully executed multiple projects across India, earning a reputation for reliability, excellence, and customer satisfaction. Whether you're looking for solar structure solutions, customized metal fabrication, or architectural designs, Sidhu Steel Solar is your trusted partner. Get in touch with us today to explore how we can assist you in achieving your goals with high-quality, innovative, and sustainable metal solutions.
